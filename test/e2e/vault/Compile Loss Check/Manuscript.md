# The Lighthouse

## Arrival

Mara reached the island on the last ferry of the season.

The path to the lighthouse was narrow, and the wind pushed at her back the whole way up.

## Storm

The storm came in before dark. Rain hit the windows in long gray sheets.

The light turned.

She counted the seconds between each flash, the way her father had taught her.

## Keeper

The keeper was an old man with salt in his beard and ink on his hands.

Later, he showed her the logbook. Every night for forty years was there, in the same careful hand.

## Lamp

At the top of the tower, the lamp was larger than she had expected.

“It never stops,” the keeper said. “Not for weather, not for me.”

## Morning

In the morning the sea was flat and bright.

Mara wrote her first entry in the logbook.
